# Comic Studio generated backgrounds

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. Generated source images were inspected, retained, and encoded as JPEGs at their original 1619 × 971 resolution for use in the website. These are imaginative educational illustrations rather than authenticated historical reconstructions.

Final project assets:

- `public/tools/comic-studio/assets/ancient-market.jpg`
- `public/tools/comic-studio/assets/future-lab.jpg`

## Ancient marketplace prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: Comic Studio background for students to place their own characters and speech bubbles. Create ONE landscape illustration, aspect ratio 5:3, of an ancient Mediterranean marketplace: sunlit stone plaza, shaded cloth stalls, clay jars, baskets of fruit, distant classical stone architecture. Original educational cartoon illustration with expressive dark ink outlines, warm parchment colors, restrained cel shading, crisp readable shapes, richer detail than simple geometric clip art. Eye-level camera with a broad empty foreground occupying the lower third for movable characters. No people, no writing, no labels, no logos, no border. This is an editable comic scene backdrop, not a finished comic panel.

## Futuristic laboratory prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: Comic Studio background for students to place their own characters and speech bubbles. Create ONE landscape illustration, aspect ratio 5:3, of a futuristic research laboratory: curved observation window showing a distant planet, elegant work benches with scientific instruments, screens containing only abstract shapes, colorful specimen containers, tiled floor. Original educational cartoon illustration with expressive dark ink outlines, muted teal and violet colors with warm accents, restrained cel shading, crisp readable shapes, richer detail than simple geometric clip art. Eye-level camera with a broad empty foreground occupying the lower third for movable characters. No people, no writing, no labels, no logos, no border. This is an editable comic scene backdrop, not a finished comic panel.
