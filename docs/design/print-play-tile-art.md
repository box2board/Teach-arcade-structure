# Print & Play tile artwork

Approved direction: Soft Arcade on Pale Sage (#E8EFE7), option C. Ten original illustrations generated with the built-in image generation tool cover five game types, The Revolutionary Spy Escape featured card, and four subject tiles. Each uses transparent 384×256 and 768×512 WebP files, navy outlines, white paper and muted slate, teal and gold accents. Prompts are recorded in print-play-soft-prompts.json.

Only hub cards and their artwork styling change. Text, navigation, metadata, scripts and downloads are preserved. Informational cards, printable previews, PDFs and product pages are unchanged. Images are decorative with empty alt text; tile links retain accessible names.
