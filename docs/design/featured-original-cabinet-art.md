Only the Featured Original Activities row changes. Original destinations, category names and descriptions are preserved. The control deck is decorative and does not introduce nested buttons.

The collection displays three columns on desktop, two on tablets, and one on phones. Focus outlines and reduced-motion behavior follow the existing homepage conventions.
