# ELA bank review notes

## Scope and source basis

These are original instructional questions for grades 7–12. The topic inventory follows the broad reading, writing, and language domains in the official Common Core State Standards for English Language Arts & Literacy, including the 6–12 strands. This is a topic-level reference, not a claim of one-to-one standards alignment or an Ohio standards crosswalk.

- Common Core State Standards Initiative, English Language Arts Standards: https://www.corestandards.org/english-language-arts-standards/
- ADA-compliant standards PDF: https://www.corestandards.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/ADA-Compliant-ELA-Standards.pdf

## Content safeguards and review results

- All sample sentences and excerpts were written for this bank; questions do not depend on external readings.
- No modern copyrighted literary excerpts, lyrics, worksheets, or commercial question-bank items were used.
- Interpretation prompts include the relevant text and ask for a text-supported response.
- Grammar questions concern standard edited English conventions; they do not treat dialect differences as evidence of intelligence or worth.
- Automated validation confirms 25 sets, 500 questions, IDs 1–20 per set, four distinct choices, 7/8/5 skill counts, and five correct answers at each option position per set.
- A near-duplicate scan found no within-set prompt pairs above 0.82 normalized edit similarity.
- Shared-loader tests verified generated copies and preserved correct answers through five rounds of runtime shuffling for every set.

A teacher review for local curriculum fit and final wording remains appropriate before publication. This work does not include a line-by-line Ohio standards mapping.
