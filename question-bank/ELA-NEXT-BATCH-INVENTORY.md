## Expansion inventory — planned

**Literature & Literary Analysis:** archetypes and allusions; comparing texts; point of view and reliability; sound devices; literary movements; satire and parody; prose style and diction.

**Reading & Informational Text:** evaluating claims; integrating information across sources; charts and multimedia; author purpose and audience; bias and perspective; distinguishing fact, opinion, and inference.

**Language, Grammar & Vocabulary:** parts of speech; pronoun reference; verb tense and voice; modifiers; parallel structure; connotation and denotation; figurative and technical word meaning; commonly confused words.

**Writing & Composition:** argument essays; informative writing; narrative writing; introductions and conclusions; organization and transitions; editing for clarity; writing for audience and purpose.

**Research, Argument & Media Literacy:** evaluating websites and authorship; corroboration; citation practices; copyright and fair use fundamentals; misinformation and source bias; visual rhetoric; ethical use of AI and digital sources.
