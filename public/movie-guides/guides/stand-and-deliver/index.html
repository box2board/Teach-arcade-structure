About the Film

Students prepare for an advanced mathematics exam under demanding conditions, then confront doubts about their results. Analyze the relationship among effort, access, trust, and recognition.

Content to Preview

  • English and Spanish profanity, sexual jokes, and inappropriate comments about female students.
  • Threats, school conflict, gang-related pressure, and a teacher’s health crisis.
  • Smoking, drinking, financial pressures, and stressful accusations of cheating.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 8–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
