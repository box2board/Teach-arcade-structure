About the Film

Five student entrepreneurs prepare to present their businesses at an international competition. Examine how evidence, resources, and personal storytelling shape a pitch and the documentary’s account of success.

Content to Preview

  • News footage of violent unrest includes artillery and a dead body; hurricane destruction is shown.
  • Accounts of poverty, civil unrest, and airport detention and deportation.
  • Adult drinking, bleeped language, and mild sexual references; the studio lists TV-14.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 8–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
