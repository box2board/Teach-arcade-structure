About the Film

Three Black women use mathematical expertise, advocacy, and collaboration to advance the space program while confronting unequal access to work, recognition, and education.

Content to Preview

  • Racial and gender discrimination, humiliating exclusion, tense arguments, and period terminology.
  • Some profanity, brief romantic scenes, and adult drinking.
  • Spaceflight danger and family or workplace stress.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 7–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
