About the Film

Young lovers seek a relationship across a family feud. This 1968 adaptation uses period settings, performance, and music to interpret Shakespeare’s tragedy.

Content to Preview

  • Brief bedroom nudity and implied sexual activity involving teenage characters.
  • Sword fights, bloody injury, deaths, and suicide by poison and stabbing.
  • Threatening family pressure and sexual jokes; emotional intensity.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 9–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
