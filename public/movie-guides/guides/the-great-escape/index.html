About the Film

Allied prisoners organize a coordinated escape under surveillance. Examine planning, interdependence, and the gap between a suspenseful film and the documented escape.

Content to Preview

  • Captivity, gunfire, fatal violence, and the murder of recaptured prisoners.
  • Claustrophobic tunnels, injury, threats, and sustained pursuit.
  • Smoking, drinking, and some language; emotionally difficult ending.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 8–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
