About the Film

Enola searches for her missing mother while another mystery exposes struggles over power and reform. Examine how clues, audience address, and independent choices shape a detective story.

Content to Preview

  • Fights, attempted killing, gunfire, drowning danger, and injury.
  • Explosives and political violence; restrictive treatment at a school.
  • Some language and loss-related distress.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 7–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
