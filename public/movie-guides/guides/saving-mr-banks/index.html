About the Film

A writer and filmmakers negotiate the adaptation of Mary Poppins while childhood memories shape the story’s emotional argument. Examine authorship, collaboration, and the limits of biographical storytelling.

Content to Preview

  • Alcohol misuse, parental illness and death, and intense childhood distress.
  • A mother’s apparent suicide attempt involving water; adult themes and some language.
  • Grief and family instability; do not require personal disclosure or treat one childhood event as a complete explanation of a person.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 9–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
