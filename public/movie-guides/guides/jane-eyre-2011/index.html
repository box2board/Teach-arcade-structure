About the Film

Jane pursues dignity and independence within institutions and relationships that constrain her. Compare the film’s reordered narrative and visual Gothic atmosphere with the novel’s voice.

Content to Preview

  • Child abuse, harsh punishment, illness, death, and intense isolation.
  • An interrupted wedding reveals a concealed marriage; sexual themes and brief nude imagery in artwork.
  • Fire, injury, and discussion of a suicide by jumping; confinement and dehumanizing treatment of mental distress.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 9–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
