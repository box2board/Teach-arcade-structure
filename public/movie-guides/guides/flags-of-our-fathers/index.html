About the Film

The film moves between combat, a wartime fundraising tour, and later memories. Examine what happens when a photograph becomes a public symbol that cannot capture the experiences of everyone involved.

Content to Preview

  • Graphic combat injuries, deaths, bodies, explosions, and disturbing descriptions of wartime harm.
  • Strong language, grief, survivor guilt, and alcohol misuse.
  • Racist treatment of Ira Hayes, a Pima Marine, and demeaning language; discuss without requiring students to repeat slurs.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 11–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
