About the Film

Birdy uses observation and humor to resist a marriage arranged for financial advantage. Analyze agency, family obligations, and the choices involved in presenting the past to modern viewers.

Content to Preview

  • Arranged marriage involving a young teenager; coercion and sexual references.
  • Menstruation, sexual innuendo, a stillbirth, distressing childbirth, and references to other deaths.
  • Caning, sword injury with blood, animal deaths, some language, crude humor, and drinking.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 8–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
