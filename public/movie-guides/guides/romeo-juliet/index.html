About the Film

Shakespeare’s lovers speak adapted original dialogue in a modern world of media, weapons, and family rivalry. Examine how visual choices and changed details shape the tragedy.

Content to Preview

  • Gun violence, killings, blood, threatening confrontations, and the lovers’ deaths by suicide.
  • Sexual references, a brief intimate scene, partial nudity, and intense kissing.
  • Drug use, smoking, grief, and harsh parental pressure.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 9–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.

Classroom Connections

ShakespeareAdaptationDramatic ironyImageryConflict & consequences

Use scene evidence to explore the topics above. Pair historical portrayals with reliable sources, literary adaptations with their texts, and animated metaphors with appropriate factual context.
