About the Film

A newcomer challenges a town’s dance ban while adults and teens negotiate grief, authority, and expression. Evaluate arguments and distinguish constructive resistance from dangerous behavior.

Content to Preview

  • Language, sexual references, implied teen sex, brief rear nudity, drinking, smoking, and drug references.
  • Abusive boyfriend behavior, fights, and dangerous stunts with vehicles and trains.
  • A fatal past crash and grief shape the conflict; religious disagreements require respectful discussion.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 9–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
