About the Film

After Nemo is taken from the reef, Marlin crosses the ocean with Dory while Nemo works with aquarium companions. The adventure explores trust, help, persistence, and protective care.

Content to Preview

  • Opening attack implies the death of Nemo’s mother and most eggs.
  • Parent-child separation, capture, sharks, jellyfish injury, and repeated intense peril.
  • Aquarium threats and a child who handles fish roughly; some toilet humor.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 3–8 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.

Classroom Connections

Trust & autonomyCooperationHelp-seekingPersistenceOcean storytelling

Use scene evidence to explore the topics above. Pair historical portrayals with reliable sources, literary adaptations with their texts, and animated metaphors with appropriate factual context.
