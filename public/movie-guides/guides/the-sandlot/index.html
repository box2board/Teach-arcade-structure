About the Film

Smalls joins a neighborhood baseball group and helps turn a local fear into a larger story of belonging. Examine mentorship, unreliable assumptions, and nostalgia’s selective view of childhood.

Content to Preview

  • Sexist and derogatory insults, bullying, and some profanity.
  • A boy deceives a lifeguard to obtain an unwanted kiss; tobacco use causes vomiting.
  • Dog pursuit, unsafe stunts, and a frightening fence collapse.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 6–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.

Classroom Connections

BelongingMentorshipRumor & evidenceNarration & nostalgiaFriendship

Use scene evidence to explore the topics above. Pair historical portrayals with reliable sources, literary adaptations with their texts, and animated metaphors with appropriate factual context.
