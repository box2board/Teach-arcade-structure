About the Film

A teacher’s approach to poetry encourages students to question convention, while family and school pressures complicate the choices they make. Analyze voice, perspective, and the limits of a simple message about freedom.

Content to Preview

  • A student dies by suicide; the method is implied and the family’s discovery is distressing.
  • Controlling parental pressure, grief, and conflict with school authority.
  • Profanity, smoking, sexual references, and romantic behavior involving unwanted contact.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 10–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.

Classroom Connections

Use scene evidence to explore the topics above. Pair historical portrayals with reliable sources, literary adaptations with their texts, and animated metaphors with appropriate factual context.
