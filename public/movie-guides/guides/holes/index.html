About the Film

Stanley’s sentence at Camp Green Lake uncovers connections among injustice, family stories, and a hidden search. Trace the film’s timelines and evaluate who controls its explanations.

Content to Preview

  • Racist violence and killing, sexual coercion, and a character’s suicide through a venomous bite.
  • Abuse at a detention camp, gun threats, fights, animal harm, and danger from heat and dehydration.
  • Some language, smoking, and children in intense distress.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 5–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
