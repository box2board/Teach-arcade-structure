About the Film

Children discuss daily life, concerns, and hopes across varied settings. Compare individual perspectives while examining the choices involved in translation, interviewing, and selecting a global portrait.

Content to Preview

  • Children discuss bullying, violence, hardship, and suicide-related distress.
  • Family separation, grief, and discussion of war and terrorism.
  • Some mature language and topics; offer an alternative without requiring explanation.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 6–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
