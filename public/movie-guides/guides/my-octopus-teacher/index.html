About the Film

A filmmaker’s repeated encounters with an octopus become a personal account of attention and connection. Separate recorded behavior from narration and examine the ethics of observation.

Content to Preview

  • Animal attacks, visible injury, predation, and the octopus’s death.
  • Underwater danger and emotionally intense narration about loss.
  • Discussion of personal exhaustion and distress; no required student disclosures.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 6–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
