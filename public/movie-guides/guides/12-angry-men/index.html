About the Film

Twelve jurors weigh a murder charge while impatience, assumptions, and conflicting interpretations challenge their deliberation. Study how a group tests reasons and changes its decisions.

Content to Preview

  • Discussion of a father’s murder, knife demonstrations, threats, and angry confrontation.
  • Prejudiced generalizations about class and background; references to family violence.
  • Smoking and some mild language.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 9–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
