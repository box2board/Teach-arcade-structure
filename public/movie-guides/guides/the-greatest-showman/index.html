About the Film

A musical presents ambition, belonging, and the costs of seeking elite approval. Analyze the songs’ arguments while checking the film’s celebratory portrait against Barnum’s documented exploitation.

Content to Preview

  • Discrimination based on race, appearance, and class; dehumanizing display themes.
  • Romantic tension, marital strain, alcohol use, fights, and a dangerous fire.
  • The celebratory narrative can obscure historical exploitation and needs explicit context.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 7–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
