About the Film

A child befriends a stranded visitor and works with his siblings to help him return home. Explore empathy, trust, communication, and the tension between care and control.

Content to Preview

  • A child and the visitor become seriously ill; an apparent death and medical intervention are distressing.
  • Government pursuit, frightening protective suits, separation, and peril.
  • Some profanity, insults, brief alcohol-related behavior, and family conflict.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 5–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.

Classroom Connections

Empathy & perspectiveCommunicationTrustFamily changeCooperation

Use scene evidence to explore the topics above. Pair historical portrayals with reliable sources, literary adaptations with their texts, and animated metaphors with appropriate factual context.
