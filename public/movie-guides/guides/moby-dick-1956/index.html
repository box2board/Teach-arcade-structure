About the Film

Ishmael joins a whaling crew whose captain’s obsession threatens the entire voyage. Study narration, symbolism, and responsibility through a condensed adaptation of Melville’s novel.

Content to Preview

  • Whale hunting, harpoons, animal injury, and intense sea peril.
  • Drowning, deaths, threatening leadership, and frightening final images.
  • Dated racial or cultural stereotypes and religious imagery.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 8–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.

Classroom Connections

Obsession & leadershipNarrationSymbolismForeshadowingAdaptation

Use scene evidence to explore the topics above. Pair historical portrayals with reliable sources, literary adaptations with their texts, and animated metaphors with appropriate factual context.
