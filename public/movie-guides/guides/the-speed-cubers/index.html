About the Film

Max Park and Feliks Zemdegs compete while sustaining a supportive relationship. Analyze how practice, community, and documentary choices complicate a simple rivalry narrative.

Content to Preview

  • Discussion of developmental challenges and family worries.
  • Competition disappointment and emotionally intense interviews.
  • Accounts of autism and family concerns; discuss the individuals respectfully without requiring personal disclosure.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 5–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
