About the Film

George Bailey’s interrupted ambitions and community ties become the focus of a fantasy about value, responsibility, and interdependence. Read its hopeful ending alongside the pressures it depicts.

Content to Preview

  • Suicidal thoughts and a crisis at a bridge; intense despair and threatened self-harm.
  • A child’s death is discussed in an alternate reality; violence, bullying, and injury.
  • Alcohol, family conflict, and dated portrayals of gender and race.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 8–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
