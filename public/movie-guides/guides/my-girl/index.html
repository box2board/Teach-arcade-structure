About the Film

Vada navigates friendship, family change, and loss during a summer of growing understanding. Explore how the film communicates feelings and what supportive responses can look like.

Content to Preview

  • A child dies following bee stings; the funeral includes a body-viewing scene and intense distress.
  • A funeral-home setting, parental death, and repeated discussion of illness and dying.
  • Puberty, mild sexual references, some language, and family conflict.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 6–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
