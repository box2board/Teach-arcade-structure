About the Film

A newly combined team confronts racial division while its players and coaches learn what shared responsibility requires. Study belonging and accountability alongside the history beyond the sports story.

Content to Preview

  • Racist hostility, derogatory language, fights, and football collisions.
  • A serious car crash, paralysis, grief, and funeral scenes.
  • Some profanity, brief kissing, and sexual references.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 7–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.

Classroom Connections

BelongingAccountabilityLeadershipSchool desegregationTeamwork

Use scene evidence to explore the topics above. Pair historical portrayals with reliable sources, literary adaptations with their texts, and animated metaphors with appropriate factual context.
