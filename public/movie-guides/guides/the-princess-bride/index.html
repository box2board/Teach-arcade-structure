About the Film

A grandfather’s reading frames a tale of love, deception, and unlikely cooperation. Examine how the film uses familiar adventure conventions while commenting on storytelling itself.

Content to Preview

  • Sword fights, threats, torture, apparent death, and a revenge killing.
  • Frightening rodents, fire, quicksand, and forced-marriage threats.
  • Some language and romantic kissing.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 6–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
