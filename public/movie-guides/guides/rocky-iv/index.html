About the Film

A boxing rivalry turns personal loss into a contest loaded with national symbolism. Study how spectacle, training, and the final fight build ideas about the United States, the Soviet Union, and change.

Content to Preview

  • Repeated boxing blows, blood, serious injury, and an on-screen death.
  • Grief, revenge, threatening dialogue, and some profanity.
  • National stereotypes and a depiction of performance-enhancing drug use; critically examine these portrayals.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 9–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
