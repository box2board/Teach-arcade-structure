About the Film

An aspiring composer becomes a music teacher and discovers how decades of relationships reshape his idea of achievement. Study mentorship, accessibility, and the tension between professional goals and family attention.

Content to Preview

  • Arguments about disability, parental frustration, and inaccessible communication.
  • A former student’s wartime death and grief.
  • Romantic tension between an adult teacher and a student; some language and smoking.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 8–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
