About the Film

When Joey is sold to the British cavalry, his bond with Albert is tested by World War I. The story follows the horse through the lives of soldiers and civilians on opposing sides of the conflict.

Content to Preview

Some scenes may be distressing, especially for students sensitive to animal suffering or wartime loss.

  • Battle violence: gunfire, explosions, soldiers killed, and bodies on the battlefield; limited bloody injury detail.
  • Animal suffering: horses injured or killed, collapse from exhaustion, and a prolonged barbed-wire entanglement.
  • Other considerations: threats with weapons, mild profanity and sexual references, and adult smoking and drinking.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing policies, and offer an alternative assignment where appropriate. Suggested grades 7–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation.
