About the Film

A small traveler accepts a dangerous burden as companions confront fear, temptation, and divided loyalties. Study quest structure, characterization, and the limits of individual power.

Content to Preview

  • Frequent fantasy combat, arrows, stabbing, decapitation or injury detail, and deaths.
  • Frightening monsters, pursuit, dark environments, and intense peril.
  • Smoking and drinking; disturbing transformation and temptation scenes.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 7–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
