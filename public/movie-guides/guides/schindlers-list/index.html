About the Film

The film follows Oskar Schindler’s movement from wartime profiteering toward efforts to protect Jewish workers. Study those choices alongside Jewish individuals’ agency and the wider history of Nazi persecution and genocide.

Content to Preview

  • Graphic executions, killings, persecution of children, bodies, and sustained genocidal violence.
  • Nudity during dehumanizing inspections and sexual situations; sexual coercion or assault.
  • Strong language, antisemitic abuse, terror, separation, and trauma. Preview the entire assigned edition.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 11–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
