About the Film

Rudy pursues a place at Notre Dame through study, work, and athletic effort. Examine persistence alongside support, opportunity, costs, and the film’s shaping of a real life.

Content to Preview

  • A workplace accident kills a friend; grief and dangerous physical activity.
  • Football impacts, injury risk, some language, drinking, and smoking.
  • Academic frustration, rejection, and family conflict; avoid ranking students’ worth by achievement.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 7–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
