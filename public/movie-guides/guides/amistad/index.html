About the Film

After Africans resist captivity aboard La Amistad in 1839, their journey toward home becomes a legal battle in the United States. The film follows their demands for freedom alongside the work of translators, abolitionists, and lawyers.

Content to Preview

This is a mature film about enslavement and racial injustice. The opening revolt and later Middle Passage sequence can be especially distressing.

  • Graphic violence: bloody killings, whipping, and people forced overboard to drown.
  • Nudity & sexual violence: male and female nonsexual nudity during captivity; implied rape.
  • Other considerations: dehumanizing treatment, family separation, racist attitudes, and some mild profanity.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 10–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, not the film’s rating.
