About the Film

A talented student develops her spelling skills through practice, mentorship, and community support while navigating grief, pressure, and uncertainty about belonging.

Content to Preview

  • Bullying, some profanity, racial or class assumptions, and tense family arguments.
  • Discussion of a deceased parent and grief; references to neighborhood crime.
  • Competition anxiety and an adult mentor’s loss.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 5–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
