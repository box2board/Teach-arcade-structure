About the Film

Two fictional FBI investigators confront racist violence and local obstruction after three civil rights workers disappear. Pair the drama with historical sources that restore the identities and organizing work of the people whose struggle lies beyond its investigative focus.

Content to Preview

  • Racist violence, murders, attempted lynching, beatings, and burning homes or churches.
  • Domestic abuse, threatening interrogation, and strong language including racial slurs.
  • Intimidation and sustained fear affecting Black residents; some scenes can be especially distressing.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 11–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
