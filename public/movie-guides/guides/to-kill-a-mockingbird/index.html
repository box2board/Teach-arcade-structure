About the Film

Scout’s childhood observations connect neighborhood assumptions with a trial shaped by racism. Analyze evidence, perspective, and the difference between an individual act of courage and a just system.

Content to Preview

  • Racial slurs, racist intimidation, and a threatened lynching.
  • Detailed discussion of an alleged rape and implications of family abuse.
  • An attack on children, death, and a dog being shot; distressing injustice.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 9–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
