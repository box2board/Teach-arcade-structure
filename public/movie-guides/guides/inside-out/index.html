About the Film

Riley’s move disrupts familiar relationships while personified emotions struggle to respond. Study how listening, expressing difficult feelings, and accepting mixed emotions change the story.

Content to Preview

  • Homesickness, family arguments, loneliness, and a child attempting to run away.
  • Frightening imaginary scenes, peril, and the loss of a beloved imaginary character.
  • Some rude humor and emotionally intense moments of sadness.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 5–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.

Classroom Connections

Emotional vocabularyEmpathyChange & belongingCommunicationPersonification

Use scene evidence to explore the topics above. Pair historical portrayals with reliable sources, literary adaptations with their texts, and animated metaphors with appropriate factual context.
