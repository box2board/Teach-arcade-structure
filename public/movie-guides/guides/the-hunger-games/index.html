About the Film

Katniss enters a televised competition designed to reinforce political control. Examine spectacle, unequal power, and how actions acquire meaning for different audiences.

Content to Preview

  • Children and teenagers are forced to fight to the death; killings, blood, and injury.
  • A young child’s death, oppressive threats, and intense pursuit.
  • Some language, alcohol use, and scenes of deprivation.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 8–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.

Classroom Connections

Power & spectacleMedia framingInequalityResistanceAdaptation

Use scene evidence to explore the topics above. Pair historical portrayals with reliable sources, literary adaptations with their texts, and animated metaphors with appropriate factual context.
