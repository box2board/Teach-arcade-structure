About the Film

Three escaped prisoners travel through an exaggerated Southern landscape of music, deception, and political spectacle. Compare a wandering quest with its literary echoes and study how tone reshapes familiar motifs.

Content to Preview

  • Gunfire, beatings, threatening pursuit, animal harm, and a threatened hanging at a Klan gathering.
  • Racist language and imagery, frequent profanity, and religious satire.
  • Drinking, smoking, and suggestive seduction scenes.

Preview your edition, follow school viewing and permission policies, and provide an appropriate alternative assignment. Suggested grades 9–12 are Teach Arcade’s classroom recommendation, separate from the film’s U.S. rating.
