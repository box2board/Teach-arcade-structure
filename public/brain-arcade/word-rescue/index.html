How to play

Choose from 20 categories and 800 total words and phrases. Multi-word answers such as Star Wars appear as separate word groups. Guess one letter at a time. Correct guesses reveal the puzzle. Every incorrect guess adds part of the trap. Easy gives you 10 misses, Medium gives you 6, and Hard gives you 4. Solve the puzzle before the trap is complete to rescue the critter.
