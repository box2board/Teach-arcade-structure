How to play

Guess one letter at a time. Correct guesses reveal the word. Each mission randomly loads one of ten rescue scenes. Correct letters visibly advance the rescue. Every incorrect guess advances the scene’s danger through one of six stages. Solve the word before the danger reaches the stranded person to complete the rescue and extend your streak.
