Red goes first.

How to Play

  • Red moves first. Take turns moving diagonally on dark squares.
  • Captures are required when available.
  • Jump again if another capture is possible.
  • Reach the far row to crown a king (moves both directions).

Classroom Tip

Have students explain their move aloud to practice strategic reasoning and respectful play.
