How to Play

  • Red moves first. In Computer mode, you are Red and the computer is Yellow.
  • In 2 Players mode, take turns moving diagonally on dark squares.
  • Captures are required when available.
  • Jump again if another capture is possible.
  • Reach the far row to crown a king (moves both directions).

Quick Tip

Look for forced captures and chain jumps before committing to your move.
