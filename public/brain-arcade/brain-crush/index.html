Brain Crush

Swap colorful gems to create match-3 combos, trigger power-ups, and climb through quick-fire levels. Brain Crush is free, fast, and built for touch-friendly classroom breaks.

How to Play
  • Swap adjacent tiles to make lines of 3 or more.
  • Match 4 to create Line Blasters, match 5 for a Color Bomb, or build T/L shapes for a Bomb.
  • Chain matches to boost your combo multiplier and score.
