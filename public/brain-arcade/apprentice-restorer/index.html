For teachers

This first region focuses on energy transfer: moving water turns a wheel, gears transfer motion, and a generator powers a circuit. Use it in pairs or as a science station. Puzzle numbers are simplified model values, not measurements from a real machine. Allow students to test unsuccessful configurations and explain what changed.

The printable notebook includes repairs, experiments, hints used, and student reflections. It stays on the current device; there is no class dashboard or online submission.
