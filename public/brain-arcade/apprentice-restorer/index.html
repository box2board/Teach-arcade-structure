Learning objective & teacher notes

Students trace energy through a connected system and use observed changes to explain how its parts work together. In this adventure, they redirect water, reconnect a channel, repair a wind pump, balance irrigation, and redirect sunlight into a ridge beacon. The wheel drives a winch that lowers a bridge. Repairs happen directly in the scene; observations appear quietly in the notebook.

Four connected restoration challenges provide practice through experimentation. After play, ask: “What stopped when the water stopped? Where did the energy come from?”
