3D Block Builder

Use this teacher-centered creative sandbox to model systems, timelines, processes, and story structures in 3D. This is a classroom ideation tool—not a gambling, picks, or predictions activity.

Privacy note: Builds are saved locally in your browser only. No accounts are required and no student data is collected by this activity.

Shortcuts
  • R / Shift+R: Rotate preview or selected block
  • Delete / Backspace: Remove selected block
  • Ctrl/Cmd+Z: Undo • Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z: Redo
  • Tap a block type, then tap the grid to place
Teacher tip: Ask students to narrate why each placed block belongs in the model to strengthen content reasoning and evidence-based thinking.

How to Use (Quick Steps)

  1. Select a category and block type from the left palette.
  2. Move your pointer over the grid and click/tap to place blocks.
  3. Rotate with keyboard shortcuts or touch buttons.
  4. Use the inspector to adjust selected blocks.
  5. Save locally, export JSON, or download a screenshot for class slides.

Classroom Ideas

  • History: Build the causes and effects of a major event as linked structures.
  • Civics: Model checks and balances with connectors and constraints.
  • Economics: Represent supply chain flow with timeline and resource blocks.
  • Science: Build a systems model of ecosystems or the water cycle.
  • ELA: Map plot structure and character decision points in 3D.
  • Interdisciplinary: Design a resilient city and justify each component.
  • SEL: Build “support systems” and discuss how constraints can be reduced.
  • Project Planning: Sequence milestones and dependencies before presentations.
