Why teachers love Slide & Snap

Slide & Snap works beautifully as a quick classroom warm-up or early-finisher station. Students practice spatial reasoning as they visualize tile moves, build executive function when planning multiple steps ahead, and strengthen persistence by sticking with a puzzle until the solution clicks. The calm, tap-to-play format makes it a focused brain break without extra distractions.

Slide & Snap FAQ

What is a sliding puzzle?

A sliding puzzle is a grid of numbered tiles with one empty space. You slide tiles into the empty spot to restore the correct order.

Can I use Slide & Snap on Chromebooks or tablets?

Yes. The game is touch-friendly and works well on Chromebooks, tablets, and laptops.

Is every puzzle solvable?

Yes. Each puzzle is shuffled using valid moves from the solved state, so it is always solvable.

How do I change the difficulty?

Choose a board size on this page or use the in-game dropdown to switch between 3×3, 4×4, 5×5, or 6×6.

Does the game track progress?

The game tracks moves and best time per board size on your device. No accounts required.
