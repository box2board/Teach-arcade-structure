How to Play
  • Tap or click a tile that sits next to the empty space.
  • Slide tiles until the numbers are in order from 1 to the final space.
  • Use the arrow keys on a keyboard to move the empty space.
  • Toggle Calm Mode to hide the timer and focus on thinking.
