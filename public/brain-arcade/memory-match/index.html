Classroom Ideas

  • Use Starter mode as a 5-minute morning bell-ringer.
  • Assign different themes by content area for station rotations.
  • Run a partner challenge where teams explain their memory strategy.
  • Have students predict moves before flipping for metacognition practice.
  • Use Teacher Mode for differentiated pairs across groups.
  • Host a weekly “personal best” reflection with growth goals.
  • Pair this game with vocabulary review by naming each emoji aloud.

Accessibility + Tips

  • Cards are keyboard focusable. Press Enter or Space to flip.
  • Press R anytime to restart with the current settings.
  • Use larger screens in landscape for Expert mode comfort.
  • If you prefer reduced motion, flip animations are minimized automatically.
  • Touch controls are optimized for tablets and Chromebooks.

Privacy note

Memory Match runs entirely in your browser. Scores are saved locally on your device only. Teach Arcade does not collect student gameplay data from this activity.

How to Play

  1. Flip two cards each turn to find matching emojis.
  2. Matched pairs stay revealed. Mismatches flip back after a short delay.
  3. Clear every pair to win. Fewer moves and faster times earn better scores.
  4. Use Teacher Controls to customize timer, hints, strikes, and sound.
