How to play

Drag or tap adjacent letters to form a word. Letters must touch horizontally, vertically, or diagonally, and you can’t reuse the same tile in one word.

In Speed Mode, words score instantly once they are valid. Submit is optional for slower play. Short words score less; longer words score more. Use Settings to adjust grid size, timer, and minimum length.
