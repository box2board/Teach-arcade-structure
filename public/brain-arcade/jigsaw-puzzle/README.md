# Brain Arcade Jigsaw Puzzle

A standalone, browser-only jigsaw game for Brain Arcade.

## Features
- Twenty original illustrated picture choices
- Six puzzle sizes: 9, 16, 36, 64, 100, and 144 pieces
- Click or tap a piece to select it, then click or tap its target square; incorrect guesses leave it on the tabletop
- Pointer and touch dragging as an alternate placement control
- Relaxed play or an optional elapsed-time display
- Picture preview, pause, restart, and change-puzzle controls
- Keyboard piece selection and movement

Full-size original WebP illustrations and 320px square thumbnails are stored in `assets/`. Puzzle state remains local to the current play session.
