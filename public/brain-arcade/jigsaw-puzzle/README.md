# Brain Arcade Jigsaw Puzzle

A standalone, browser-only jigsaw game for Brain Arcade.

## Features
- Thirty original illustrated picture choices
- Six puzzle sizes: 9, 16, 36, 64, 100, and 144 pieces
- Click or tap a piece to select it, then click or tap its target square; incorrect guesses leave it on the tabletop
- Pointer and touch dragging as an alternate placement control
- Relaxed play or an optional elapsed-time display
- Picture preview, pause, restart, and change-puzzle controls
- Keyboard piece selection and movement

Full-size original WebP illustrations and 320px square thumbnails are stored in `assets/`. Puzzle state remains local to the current play session.


## Additional picture choices

- **Redwood Forest** — A towering redwood forest with a fern-lined creek, footbridge, deer, birds and sunbeams.
- **Ancient Greek Harbor** — A coastal Greek town festival with a trireme, pottery market, musicians, olive trees and a hilltop temple.
- **Grand Canyon Rafting** — A river journey through layered red-rock canyons with rafts, hikers, wildlife and desert flowers.
- **Wild West Frontier** — An 1880s frontier town with a stagecoach, blacksmith, general store, horses, windmill and mesas.
- **Tropical Volcano Island** — A lush island with a smoking volcano, waterfall, turquoise lagoon, palms, parrots and sea turtles.
- **Deep Sea Exploration** — A research submarine explores a shipwreck among glowing fish, coral gardens, rays and a distant squid.
- **Geyser Basin** — An erupting geyser beside colorful mineral springs, boardwalks, wildflowers, bison and mountain pines.
- **Rooftop Garden** — Neighbors and students grow vegetables, fruit trees and flowers in a lively urban rooftop garden.
- **Wildlife Sanctuary** — A conservation center with otters, foxes, turtles, birds, wetland paths and caretakers.
- **Lantern Festival** — A mountain town glows with floating lanterns, riverside reflections, music, markets and evening festivities.
