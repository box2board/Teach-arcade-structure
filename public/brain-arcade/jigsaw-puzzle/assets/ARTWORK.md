# Jigsaw puzzle artwork

Ten original images generated with the built-in image generation tool. Full-resolution WebP files and 320px thumbnails live in this directory. These are imaginative illustrations, not scientific or historical reference diagrams.

## Shared prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: full square artwork for an online jigsaw puzzle, one single image. Style: polished richly detailed painterly illustration, vibrant harmonious colors, clear silhouettes, appealing to teenagers and adults. Composition: edge-to-edge square scene with interesting varied details in every quadrant and along all edges, minimal empty sky or uniform areas; coherent perspective and lighting. No jigsaw cuts, no puzzle piece outlines, no frames, no collage, no text, no logos, no watermark. This is the illustration itself, not a picture of a puzzle.
