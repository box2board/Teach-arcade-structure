Why jigsaw puzzles belong in Brain Arcade

Jigsaw puzzles are a reliable way to strengthen visual-spatial thinking and patience. They encourage students to spot patterns, compare shapes, and build a plan for assembling the image. In classrooms, jigsaw puzzles work well as station activities, early-finisher options, or calm brain breaks that keep learners engaged without distractions.

When this game launches, teachers will be able to select an appropriate difficulty level, set expectations for quiet focus, and invite students to collaborate or work independently. The experience is designed to be classroom-friendly and lightweight while still delivering meaningful cognitive benefits.
