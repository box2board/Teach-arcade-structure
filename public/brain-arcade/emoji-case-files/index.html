Emoji Case Files FAQ

What is Emoji Case Files?

Emoji Case Files is a noir-inspired phrase investigation game. Decode sayings by placing the right emoji evidence into each blank slot.

How does Integrity work?

Integrity starts at 100 in every case. Wrong placement attempts cost 10, wrong submits cost 15, and if integrity reaches zero the case fails.

How do I unlock new files?

Complete at least 8 of 10 cases in your current file to unlock the next file.
