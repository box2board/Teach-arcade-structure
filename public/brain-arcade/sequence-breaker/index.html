How it Works

Each round shows a set of tiles that follow a rule—except for one impostor.

  • Scan the row for the hidden pattern (numbers, colors, shapes, or directions).
  • Tap the tile that breaks the rule before time runs out.
  • Build a streak, track accuracy, and beat your best score.
