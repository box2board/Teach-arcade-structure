Submit a Resource or Classroom Request

This page is for educators who want to recommend classroom-ready resources, request a new Teach Arcade activity, or report a gap in existing content. We value practical submissions that help teachers plan faster and teach with confidence.

What this form is for

  • Free resource recommendations (no paywall or account wall preferred).
  • Requests for new game-based review topics.
  • Suggestions to improve existing resources for clarity or accessibility.
  • Corrections for content quality or broken page experiences.

What we do not accept

  • Affiliate-only landing pages, aggressive marketing pages, or doorway pages.
  • Resources that require student personal data collection.
  • Copyrighted materials submitted without permission.
  • Requests unrelated to classroom teaching and learning.

How submissions are processed

  1. Submit details with title, link, subject, and classroom context.
  2. Editorial review checks instructional value, clarity, and safety fit.
  3. Follow-up may include publication, revision request, or decline notice.

Most submission updates are shared within 3–7 school days. Large requests or complex review items may take longer, but we keep contributors informed when possible.

FAQ

Do I need to submit only original resources?

You may submit your own work or recommend existing public resources that are classroom-appropriate and properly attributed.

Can I submit district-specific requests?

Yes, but include enough context (grade, unit, constraints) so we can determine whether it fits broad educator use.

Will every submission be published?

No. We prioritize instructional usefulness, clarity, and student-safe implementation.

Can I submit without a URL?

Yes. Idea submissions are welcome when you clearly describe the classroom problem and desired format.

How will I know if you accepted my submission?

If you provide an email, we may send a status update or follow-up question after review.

Can I send multiple submissions?

Absolutely. Separate submissions by topic often speed review.

Safety and privacy note: do not include student-identifying information. See our Privacy Policy for data handling details.
