How to Use These Adventures

Each adventure is:

  • Designed for ~10–15 minutes of play time.
  • Perfect for pairs or small groups at a single device.
  • Built around branching decisions with multiple possible outcomes.
  • Easy to debrief with whole-class discussion after the ending.
