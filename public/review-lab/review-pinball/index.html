How to play

Flippers: ← / →, A / D, or the on-screen buttons.
Launch: hold Space or PLUNGER, then release. A short pull may roll back. Press P to pause.

Earn a ball: after a drain, answer two questions correctly. Your score stays, and you launch the next ball yourself.

Jackpot: light the four bank targets, then hit a bumper. The reactor’s four light segments show your progress.

Ramp bonus: every third completed ramp earns 2,000. Alternate a ramp and loop within 8 seconds for a 1,000 combo.

Rush: hit the ramp, loop and turbine to earn 15 seconds of double shot points. The three small inserts track these shots.

Cosmic shots: both ramps count toward the ramp bonus. The moon crater holds the ball briefly, awards 500, then ejects it. RIGHT / → / D also operates the upper flipper.
