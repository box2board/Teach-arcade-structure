How does displacement measure volume?

Read the bottom of the water’s meniscus at eye level before and after fully submerging the sample. The rise in volume equals the sample volume in this model: final reading − initial reading. One milliliter is one cubic centimeter.

Density = dry mass ÷ displaced volume. Dividing grams by milliliters gives g/mL, numerically equal to g/cm³. The app supports the calculation; you can check one yourself at the end.

A wet sample would add water to the measured mass. A bubble stuck to the sample would displace extra water. The guide helps you follow the procedure, but this simulation does not model bubbles or residual surface water.
