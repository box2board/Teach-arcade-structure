Why repeat the measurement? What is being controlled?

Three readings help you see whether the result is consistent. We keep voltage, instruments, and circuit connections the same. Only nominal resistance changes. Small supply variation, meter noise, and a fixed resistor tolerance are modeled; repeated displayed readings can still be identical.

The meters are connected and zeroed automatically after assembly. Current is measured in series; voltage is measured across the load. This introductory version saves readings and calculates means and power for you.
