Which ground cover carries away the least soil?

Why filter, dry, and subtract?

Cloudy water suggests suspended material, but color is not a mass measurement. The dry filter traps the transported soil particles in this model.

A wet filter includes water mass. Drying removes that water; subtracting the empty dry filter’s mass leaves an estimate of collected sediment mass.

We keep soil type, starting mass, initial moisture, tray area, slope, rainfall volume, rainfall duration, source height, and handling the same. Ground cover is the changed treatment. Planted cover includes both shoots and roots; this test does not isolate their individual effects.
