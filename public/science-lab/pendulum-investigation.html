What is one full swing? Why time ten?

Start on one side, pass the center, reach the opposite side, and return to the original side. That is one complete cycle. A trip from one side to the other is only half a cycle.

Period is time per complete cycle: total elapsed time ÷ number of cycles. Timing ten cycles spreads a start/stop timing error over ten cycles. Repeats help reveal variability; they do not eliminate every source of error.

Length is measured from the pivot to the center of the bob, not just along the visible string. The prepared lengths are exact in this model.

Optional calculation: estimate gravity
