About this classroom simulation

Explore career interests, compare education models, and manage five simulated working years. Salary figures, training costs, taxes, expenses, and outcomes are illustrative game assumptions—not current career data or personalized financial guidance. The working years begin after your chosen training is complete. Interest questions suggest careers to explore; they do not measure aptitude or determine eligibility.

Teachers can adjust the challenge and available careers before a run. Pause at each year review to discuss opportunity cost, financial stability, and well-being. Progress stays in this browser; it is not sent to a teacher dashboard.
