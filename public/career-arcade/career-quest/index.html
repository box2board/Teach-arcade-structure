Career Quest

Scenario-based career and money decisions—see where your choices lead.

Career Quest helps students practice practical decision-making before they face these choices in real life. In each run, students explore career fit, choose an education path, manage stress and performance, and adapt to workplace or financial curveballs over five simulated years.

Teachers can quickly tune the challenge level, cost-of-living pressure, and career availability to match class goals. Because the simulation is anonymous and browser-based, it works well for stations, advisory periods, or whole-group discussion with minimal setup.
