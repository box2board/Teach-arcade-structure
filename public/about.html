About Teach Arcade

Teach Arcade is a free, organized hub for teacher-made and open educational resources—built to make classroom planning faster and student practice more engaging.

Browse Subjects Play Review Games Submit a Resource

What Teach Arcade provides

  • Classroom-ready review games and interactive practice
  • Curated teacher resources and open educational materials (OER)
  • Organized subject pages to reduce searching and clicking
  • Student-friendly activities for stations and bell-ringers

How to use it

  • Teachers: browse by subject and run activities instantly
  • Students: practice in class or at home
  • Contributors: submit high-quality classroom resources

Quality & organization

Teach Arcade prioritizes clear labeling and organization so you can quickly find activities that fit your grade level, topic, and time constraints.

Transparency

Teach Arcade links to teacher-made and open resources. Some pages may include ads to support hosting and development. Submissions must be classroom-appropriate.

Contact & feedback

Want a new subject, game type, or need to report an issue? Use the Submit page to reach out.
