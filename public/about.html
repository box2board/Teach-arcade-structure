About Teach Arcade

Teach Arcade is a free, organized hub for teacher-made and open educational resources—built to make classroom planning faster and student practice more engaging.

Browse Subjects Play Review Games Submit a Resource

Focus: quick classroom wins—review games, practice activities, and curated links that teachers can trust. Everything is organized by subject and designed to be easy to use on phones, Chromebooks, and classroom devices.

What Teach Arcade provides

  • Classroom-ready review games and interactive practice
  • Curated teacher resources and open educational materials (OER)
  • Organized subject pages to reduce searching and clicking around
  • Student-friendly experiences designed for stations and bell-ringers

How to use it

  • Teachers: browse by subject, pick an activity, and run it instantly
  • Students: play in class or at home for extra practice
  • Contributors: submit high-quality resources to help the community

Quality and organization

Teach Arcade prioritizes clear labeling and organization so you can quickly find what fits your grade level, topic, and time constraints. When possible, resources are reviewed for basic functionality and classroom fit.

Transparency

Teach Arcade links to teacher-made and open resources. Some pages may include ads to support hosting and development. Submissions should be classroom-appropriate and credited when required.

Contact / feedback

Want a new subject page, a new game type, or to report a broken link? Use the Submit page to send a note.
