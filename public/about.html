More options. Less prep.

The same review routine can lose its spark. Teach Arcade grew from a simple idea: give teachers more ways to get students involved without adding hours of preparation.

Games invite participation. Stories encourage decisions. Tools make classroom routines easier. You choose what fits your lesson.
