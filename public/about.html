About Teach Arcade

Teach Arcade brings free classroom review games, interactive learning experiences, and practical teacher tools together in one place. Created by a classroom teacher, it helps educators turn practice into something students want to participate in.

Explore Review Games Find Teacher Tools Visit Brain Arcade

Built by a teacher

Behind Teach Arcade is a classroom teacher with more than 15 years of teaching experience and a master’s degree in educational technology. Experience teaching middle and high school students shapes the focus: useful activities, clear directions, and less preparation for teachers.

Why Teach Arcade exists

Teachers put a lot of time into finding ways to help students practice. Repeating the same review routine can make it difficult to keep students involved. Teach Arcade grew from the desire to give teachers more options—games that invite participation, experiences that encourage problem solving, and tools that make everyday classroom tasks easier.

What you’ll find here

Arcade Review Games

Practice academic content through games that combine questions with challenges, competition, and progression.

Interactive Learning Experiences

Explore escape rooms and decision-based activities that ask students to use what they know, work through clues, and make choices.

Teacher Tools

Support everyday instruction with timers, group makers, quiz buzzers, graphic organizers, and other practical classroom tools.

Brain Arcade

Enjoy puzzles, creative building, and recreational games for brain breaks and early-finisher time. These activities focus on play rather than subject-specific review.

Make it fit your classroom

Use a review game with the whole class, build an activity into a station, or choose a topic for independent practice. Brain Arcade offers another option for a short break or early finishers. Pick the experience that fits your students, your lesson, and the time you have.

Help shape what comes next

Teach Arcade continues to grow. Have an idea for a topic or activity, or found something that needs fixing? Teacher and student feedback helps guide improvements and new additions.
