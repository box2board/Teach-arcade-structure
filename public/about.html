About Teach Arcade

Teach Arcade is a free, organized hub for teacher-made and open educational resources—built to make classroom planning faster and student practice more engaging.

Browse Subjects Play Review Games Submit a Resource

What Teach Arcade provides

  • Classroom-ready review games and interactive practice
  • Curated teacher resources and open educational materials (OER)
  • Organized subject pages to reduce searching and clicking
  • Student-friendly activities for stations and bell-ringers

How to use it

  • Teachers: browse by subject and run activities instantly
  • Students: practice in class or at home
  • Contributors: submit high-quality classroom resources

Quality & organization

Teach Arcade prioritizes clear labeling and organization so you can quickly find activities that fit your grade level, topic, and time constraints.

Transparency

Teach Arcade links to teacher-made and open resources. Some pages may include ads to support hosting and development. Submissions must be classroom-appropriate.

Contact & feedback

Want a new subject, game type, or need to report an issue? Use the Contact page or the Submit form to reach out.

Our teaching philosophy

Teach Arcade was created to make learning feel active and memorable. We believe students learn best when they can practice a skill in multiple ways: guided instruction, collaborative discussion, independent problem solving, and immediate feedback. Our tools are intentionally lightweight so they work on shared devices, load quickly, and keep the focus on learning objectives instead of flashy distractions.

The site prioritizes teacher time. Every game or tool is designed to be used in a real classroom with minimal setup, clear directions, and flexible pacing. You can launch a resource in minutes, differentiate with partners or stations, and wrap up with reflection prompts or exit tickets that connect back to standards.

How we curate content

Teach Arcade combines original activities with vetted, teacher-created resources. We review submissions for classroom-appropriate language, accuracy, and ease of use. When we link to external materials, we choose sources that are reliable, age-appropriate, and useful for lesson planning. If a resource no longer fits our quality guidelines, we update or remove it.

We also gather feedback from educators to improve navigation and add missing topics. This keeps the collection aligned with real classroom needs, not just search trends. Teachers can recommend topics through the contact form, and we use those requests to guide upcoming additions.

Advertising & sustainability

Teach Arcade is free to use. We keep costs down with efficient code and lightweight design, and we may display non-intrusive ads to cover hosting and development. We do not use popups or interstitial ads that disrupt learning, and we avoid collecting sensitive student data.

If you notice an ad placement that interferes with instruction, please let us know so we can adjust the experience for classroom use.
