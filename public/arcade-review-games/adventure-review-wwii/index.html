Learning goals & teacher notes

Use after instruction to review selected WWII military events. Students sequence events, distinguish theaters, and explain connections. Movement speed does not affect learning results. Summaries are written for this activity and link to historical sources.

This interactive archive uses a schematic map. It is not a complete WWII unit and does not cover all major civilian experiences or the Holocaust. The final written explanation requires teacher review. Downloaded results are local practice summaries; they are not sent to a teacher dashboard.

Sources: U.S. Holocaust Memorial Museum timeline · National WWII Museum Pacific strategy · Normandy
