# Volcano Ascent background

`volcanic-dusk.webp` was generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then encoded as a 49 KB WebP for the game. The renderer adds a dark wash and slow, bounded parallax; the original procedural scenery remains the loading/error fallback.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: subtle panoramic background for a 2D side-scrolling educational volcano expedition game, 1536x1024 landscape. A distant volcanic landscape with layered basalt ridgelines, one broad volcanic peak and soft wisps of ash drifting across a muted dusk sky. Painterly polished game environment art, restrained detail, charcoal blue-gray silhouettes with dusty warm brown and very faint amber at the horizon. Atmospheric depth, low contrast and low saturation throughout; quiet open sky in upper half. The gameplay platforms, ladders and orange explorer will be drawn over this background and must remain easy to distinguish. Background only, no foreground platforms, no characters, no ladders, no interface, no text or watermark. Avoid bright lava rivers and dramatic explosions.
