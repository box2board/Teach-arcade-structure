Scientific Method Review

Scientific Method Maze turns the research process into an ordered mission. Students move through Question, Hypothesis, Test, Analyze, and Conclusion stations while answering scientific-method questions and applying each concept to investigation scenarios.

Use it as an individual review, a small-group station, or a projected whole-class activity. The difficulty setting changes the challenge while the Foundation → Application structure keeps the academic sequence visible throughout the run.
