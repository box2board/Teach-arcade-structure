How to Play

  1. Teams control territories across the arena.
  2. Choose your territory, then attack only a highlighted adjacent enemy.
  3. Answer correctly to capture it.
  4. After a capture, Bank to keep the streak or Push to attack again.
  5. Banking ends the turn and keeps every capture.
  6. A failed Push returns the streak’s most recent capture to its previous owner.
  7. Special territories grant a simple bonus, shield, or extra value.
  8. Win by territory points when time ends, reaching the target, or eliminating every rival.
