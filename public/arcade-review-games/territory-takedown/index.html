How to Play

Take a turn

  1. The active team chooses a glowing tile.
  2. Ask the displayed question or your own Quick Play prompt.
  3. Control the timer, reveal the answer if prepared, then mark Correct or Incorrect.
  4. A correct answer captures the tile or damages a fortress. Play advances automatically.

Expansion & comeback

Normally, teams choose adjacent tiles. Open Expansion allows any unclaimed tile. A team without a home base receives a Comeback Question; success restores a protected base on an open tile, or challenges a non-base border tile.

Special territories

🏰 takes two hits · ⚡ awards an ability · ◆ adds bonus points · ↔ opens a friendly challenge · ★ triggers a positive random event · ⌂ marks a home base.

Power-ups

Shield blocks one capture; Extra Turn acts again; Long Range reaches two spaces; Double Capture claims a connected open tile; Blockade pauses one territory; Reinforce adds defense; Score Boost doubles capture points; Second Chance retries an incorrect answer.
