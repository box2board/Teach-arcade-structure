Race through the digital school arcade and power up with every correct math fact.
How to Play

Run, jump, and speed through the level while solving addition facts.

  • Hit the correct answer platform, box, or door to gain points and speed.
  • Wrong answers only slow you down and cost a few points — you can’t fail.
  • You have 5 minutes. Score as high as you can!
Controls
  • Move: Left / Right arrows (or on-screen buttons)
  • Jump: Up arrow / Space (tap twice to double jump)
