Math Rush Genesis: Addition
Sprint through the level and clear 10 addition gates as fast as you can.
How to Play

Run, jump, and dodge hazards to reach glowing math gates.

  • Cross a math gate to trigger a question.
  • Movement pauses until you answer on the number pad.
  • Correct = speed boost + points. Wrong = slowdown & point penalty.
  • The clock counts up. Beat your best time for 10 questions.
Controls
  • Move: Left / Right arrows (or on-screen buttons)
  • Jump: Up arrow / Space (or Jump button)
  • Answer: Tap number pad or use number keys (auto-submit)
