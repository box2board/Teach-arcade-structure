Free Mood & Tone ELA Arcade Game for Middle School

Undertext Rifts is a free ELA review game from Teach Arcade that helps middle school students practice mood and tone in a Stranger Things–inspired setting. Students explore a glitchy, Upside-Down style school, flipping between the normal Classroom World and the eerie Undertext World to reach glowing rifts.

Each rift presents a short reading passage and a multiple-choice question asking students to identify either the mood (the feeling created for the reader) or the tone (the author’s or narrator’s attitude). As students select correct answers, they stabilize each rift and build their score.

  • Practice identifying mood and tone in short, accessible passages.
  • Engaging 8-bit visuals with an Upside-Down, sci-fi horror classroom vibe.
  • Perfect for bell ringers, small-group stations, or individual practice.
  • No logins required — just open the page and play.

Use this game as a quick skills review before reading a longer text, after a mini-lesson on mood and tone, or as a fun Friday station in your ELA classroom.
