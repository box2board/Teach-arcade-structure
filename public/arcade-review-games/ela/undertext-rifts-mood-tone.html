How to Play

Objective: Walk the school hallway in two worlds, collect question orbs, and earn as many points as possible answering mood & tone questions.

  • Arrow keys / WASD to move
  • F key or Flip button to switch worlds
  • Golden orbs = mood & tone questions
  • Purple shadow orbs & glitch tiles deduct points but never end the game

Reach the glowing exit door whenever you're ready to see your score and rank.
