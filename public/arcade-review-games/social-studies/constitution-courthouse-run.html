Classroom use & controls

Play independently, in pairs, or on a projector. Easy has three rooms and Medium has five rooms with branching paths. Both have no time limit. Stone columns, archive shelves, and signal conduits shape the route. An optional Explorer token rewards a detour through the Archive with 50 bonus points. The completion screen separates review points from treasure bonuses. Easy has six Constitution review questions across three two-question reward chests: earn the archive key, seal crystal, and power cell. The hammer stays a loose pickup to keep the puzzles straightforward. Medium has twelve questions across five reward chests. Earn a hammer in the Workshop south of the Archive, revisit Switch Hall to open its north vault and south library, then earn a lantern to reveal the final chamber’s code. Your journal keeps the clue visible for the return journey.

Walk over loose tools, crystals, and keys to collect them automatically. Walk into locked doors with the matching key or into cracked walls with your hammer to open them. Stand next to chests, signs, and switches and face them to interact. Push blocks by walking into them. Undo reverses movement and block pushes. Reset puzzle returns you to the entrance and restores the block while keeping earned keys, tools, collectibles, and completed questions. Pause or switch browser tabs to stop movement.
