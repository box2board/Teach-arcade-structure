Classroom use & controls

Six Scientific Method questions across three reward chests. Explore four stations around a courtyard, earn a key and beacon supplies, and solve a light crossing and block gate. There is no time limit.

Walk into a mirror or crate to push it. After pushing the mirror onto its socket, press Interact while still facing it to rotate it. Light is harmless. Use CROSS when the receiver glows. Raised crossings and earned rewards stay saved during puzzle resets. Stand beside chests and signs, face them, then interact.
