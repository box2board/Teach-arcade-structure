Classroom use & controls

Choose Scientific Method or U.S. Constitution before difficulty. Easy has six review questions; Medium has eight; Hard has twelve. Each topic keeps its own saved adventure and appears in the results report. Explore four stations, earn supplies, direct light, and balance counterweights. Medium and Hard also require a review-earned crank to open a drawbridge. New adventures choose among three puzzle layouts. Follow each room’s signs for the current arrangement. Continue and puzzle reset keep that layout. There is no time limit.

Walk into a mirror or crate to push it. Move the mirror onto its socket, then interact to rotate it. Light is harmless. Use CROSS when the receiver glows. Keep a crate on every amber switch to hold the south gate open. In Medium and Hard, earn the crank from the Mechanic chest, then use LIFT. Opened crossings and earned rewards survive puzzle resets.
