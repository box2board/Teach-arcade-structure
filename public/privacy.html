Privacy Policy

Teach Arcade is a teacher-focused resource site built to support instruction with tools, games, and curated materials. We are committed to protecting student privacy and keeping data practices simple. We do not sell student data and we avoid collecting personally identifiable information from students.

Information we collect

We collect limited, non-sensitive information to keep the site running smoothly and to understand general usage patterns. This includes anonymized analytics data such as pages visited, device type, browser version, and approximate geographic region. This data helps us improve site navigation, fix broken links, and prioritize which tools and subjects teachers use most.

Teacher submissions

If you submit a resource or send us feedback, we collect the information you provide in the form (such as your name, email, or a link to a resource). We use this information only to respond to your request or to evaluate the submission for inclusion on the site. We do not share submission details with third parties.

Advertising and analytics

Teach Arcade may display non-intrusive ads to support hosting and development. Ad partners may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver relevant ads. We do not allow popunders or forced interstitials that interrupt teaching. We use analytics tools to measure site performance and engagement, and those tools are configured to minimize personal data collection.

Student privacy commitments

Many of our resources are used in classrooms. We design the site so students can access activities without creating accounts. We encourage teachers to supervise student use and avoid entering student names in any open text fields that are visible to the public. If you believe student data has been submitted inadvertently, contact us so we can remove it.

Cookies and local storage

Some tools use local storage to save settings on a device (for example, a timer or game preference). This data stays in the browser and is not transmitted to Teach Arcade servers. You can clear this storage at any time through your browser settings.

External links

Teach Arcade links to external educational resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites. We encourage teachers to review external policies before assigning resources to students.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at support@teacharcade.com.
