Print & Play Games are Teach Arcade’s offline classroom games—designed for stations, review days, sub plans, and anytime you want students learning without screens. Each game is built to be easy to prep, clear to run, and flexible across grade levels.

Teachers can choose from escape-style puzzle missions, fast-paced card games, printable board games, and decision-based scenarios that reinforce key content skills like evidence-based reasoning, vocabulary mastery, cause-and-effect, and historical or scientific thinking.

This hub is organized by game type first (so you can quickly choose the format you need), followed by subject browsing to match your curriculum. All Print & Play games on Teach Arcade are free and will expand regularly.
