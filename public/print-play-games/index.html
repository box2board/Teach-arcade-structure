Classroom-ready printable games—escape-style puzzles, crosswords, card games, board games, and decision scenarios you can run in minutes. No devices required.

Print & Play Games are Teach Arcade’s offline classroom games—designed for stations, review days, sub plans, and anytime you want students learning without screens. Each game is built to be easy to prep, clear to run, and flexible across grade levels.

Teachers can choose from printable puzzles, escape-style missions, card games, board games, and decision-based scenarios that reinforce content knowledge and classroom skills.

This hub is organized by game type first, followed by subject browsing to match your curriculum. All Print & Play games on Teach Arcade are free and will expand regularly.
