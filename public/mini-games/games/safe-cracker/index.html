45 SECOND CHALLENGE

Break into the bonus vault

Turn the dial with Left and Right. Follow the required direction, then press Space when the needle touches the moving mint arc. Three pins open a vault. Misses raise the alarm; lockdown resets the pins and costs 20 tickets.

Left / Right turn · Space sets · Aligned hits count at any speed
