Contact Teach Arcade

We love hearing from teachers. Use the information below to report an issue, request a new resource, or suggest a classroom tool. We read every message and use feedback to prioritize updates and new content.

How to reach us

  • Email: support@teacharcade.com
  • Resource submissions: Submit a resource

What to include

To help us respond quickly, please include your grade level, subject, and a short description of the classroom goal. If you are reporting a broken link or bug, share the URL and a screenshot or brief description of what happened.

Response time

We aim to respond within 2–3 school days. During heavy planning seasons, it may take a little longer, but we will follow up as soon as possible.
