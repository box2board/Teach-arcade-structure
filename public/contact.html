Contact Teach Arcade

Teach Arcade is built for teachers who need practical, classroom-ready support. Use this page for resource requests, correction reports, technical issues, and instructional feedback. Every message is reviewed by a real person and helps shape what we publish next.

Who this page is for

This contact channel is intended for classroom teachers, instructional coaches, school leaders, and support staff using Teach Arcade in educational settings. If you are sharing student-facing implementation questions, include grade level and class context so we can provide useful guidance.

What you can request

  • Broken link or technical bug reports.
  • Suggestions for new subject resources or game formats.
  • Clarification on classroom use and adaptation ideas.
  • Content corrections (accuracy, clarity, or accessibility concerns).

What we do not accept on this channel

  • Student personal data or identifiable student records.
  • Requests for custom district contracts through this inbox.
  • Mass promotional submissions unrelated to classroom instruction.

How our response workflow works

  1. Step 1: Submit your message to support@teacharcade.com with subject, grade band, and request details.
  2. Step 2: Review we triage your request by urgency (site issue, correction, or content suggestion).
  3. Step 3: Follow-up we reply with either a direct fix, a clarification, or a timeline update.

Expected response time

Most messages receive a reply within 2–4 school days. During peak planning periods, response may take longer, but we still review submissions in order and prioritize urgent technical issues first.

FAQ

Can I request a resource for a specific standard?

Yes. Share the standard language, grade level, and unit context so we can recommend a standards-friendly implementation path.

Can students contact Teach Arcade directly?

This inbox is intended for educators. Teachers should mediate student questions and avoid sending student-identifying information.

How do I report a broken activity link?

Include the exact URL and what happened (error, blank screen, or unexpected redirect). A screenshot is helpful but optional.

Can I submit multiple ideas at once?

Yes. A single email with a short bullet list is preferred; it helps us prioritize efficiently.

Do you provide guaranteed implementation timelines?

No fixed guarantee is offered, but we do provide realistic updates based on scope and current queue.

Where should I submit full resource recommendations?

Use the Submit page for formal resource submissions and idea intake.

Safety and privacy note: please avoid sharing student names or sensitive data. Review our Privacy Policy for details on how submissions are handled.
