How to Use in Class

  • Use the organizer before debates or persuasive writing assignments.
  • Ask students to label which points are strongest evidence-based reasons.
  • Turn the list into a decision-making discussion or exit ticket.

Pros and Cons FAQs

Can I rename the headers?

Yes. Edit them to match your prompt, such as "Benefits" and "Drawbacks".

Does this work for science labs?

Yes. Use it to weigh methods, variables, or experimental design choices.
