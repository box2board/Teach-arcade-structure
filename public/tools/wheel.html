Spin the Wheel: A Teacher-Friendly Random Picker for Fair Classroom Choices

If you’ve ever tried to “randomly” call on students and immediately heard, “You always pick the same people,” this wheel spinner solves that problem fast. The visual spin makes your selection feel transparent, and the excitement keeps attention up—especially during review, stations, or discussion days.

To use the wheel, type or paste one item per line: student names, team numbers, discussion prompts, vocabulary words, lab roles, or quick formative-check questions. Pick a color palette, spin, and let the wheel choose. Turn on Remove winner after spin when you want everyone to get a turn before repeats.

Try it for a discussion leader, station rotations, a random problem type, or the group that shares first. In ELA, spin for a claim/evidence sentence starter. In math, spin for a strategy a student must explain. In science, spin for lab roles or variables to test. In social studies, spin for a perspective, region, or primary source to analyze.

Want to keep the pace tight? Pair the wheel with the Classroom Timer. If you prefer list-only randomization, try the Text Randomizer.

For differentiation, keep multiple wheels: a foundations wheel and a challenge wheel. Students can earn a spin on the challenge wheel after demonstrating mastery, which makes differentiation feel like a reward instead of a label.

How to Use This in Class

What this tool does: The Wheel Spinner gives teachers a fast, transparent way to make random classroom selections without student accounts. Use it for names, teams, questions, roles, stations, vocabulary, prompts, or classroom jobs.

Quick Classroom Ideas

  • Choose a student or team to respond.
  • Assign station rotations or classroom roles.
  • Select review questions, vocabulary, or problem types.
  • Run a whole-class projected activity in Presentation Mode.
  • Remove winners after each spin so everyone gets a turn.

Suggested Grade Levels & Timing

The wheel works across grade levels and can be used for a single 30-second choice or throughout an entire lesson.

FAQ

Do students need accounts?

No. The wheel runs directly in the browser with no login required.

Can I use this with limited devices?

Yes. It is especially useful projected for whole-class instruction.

Can I reuse a class list?

Yes. Use Save List to store it in the browser and Restore to bring it back later.
