Teaching the U.S. Constitution

Use these compact points to frame the game, clarify vocabulary, or guide a short debrief.

Purpose and popular sovereignty

The Constitution establishes a framework for government whose authority comes from the people while limiting how that authority may be used.

Three branches

Legislative, executive, and judicial powers are divided among institutions with different responsibilities.

Checks and balances

Each branch has tools that can limit the others, helping prevent one part of the federal government from controlling all power.

Amendments and rights

The amendment process allows constitutional change, while the Bill of Rights protects specific civil liberties and legal safeguards.

Federalism

Power is divided between the national and state governments, creating overlapping responsibilities and continuing debate.
