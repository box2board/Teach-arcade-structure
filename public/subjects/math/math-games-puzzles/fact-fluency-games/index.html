Building addition fact fluency

Use these points to frame instruction, discussion, or a quick debrief.

Make ten

Decompose an addend to complete a ten, then add what remains.

Use known facts

Build from doubles, near doubles, and facts with zero or one instead of counting every quantity.

See relationships

Connect addition facts to related subtraction facts and notice how sums change when an addend changes.

Accuracy before speed

Encourage efficient strategies and accurate recall first; let automaticity develop through short, spaced practice.

Try This in Class

Before a timed round, name one strategy to watch for. Afterward, choose a fact from the game and have students explain two ways to find the sum.
