Teaching linear relationships

Use these points to frame instruction, discussion, or a quick debrief.

Representations

Connect a constant rate of change across a table, graph, equation, and verbal situation.

Slope

Interpret slope as rise over run and as the rate at which one quantity changes with another.

Equations

In y = mx + b, connect m to slope and b to the initial value or y-intercept.

Compare relationships

Use graphs, tables, and equations to distinguish proportional relationships from linear relationships with a nonzero initial value.

Try This in Class

After a game question, ask students to show the same relationship in a second representation—for example, sketch the graph of an equation or write an equation from two points.
