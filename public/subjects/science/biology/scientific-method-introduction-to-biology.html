Teaching the scientific method

Use these compact points to frame the game, clarify vocabulary, or guide a short debrief.

Questions and observations

An investigation begins with an observation that leads to a focused question that evidence can address.

Testable hypotheses

A hypothesis is a testable explanation or prediction, not simply a guess or a proven fact.

Variables and measurement

Identify what changes, what is measured, and what stays controlled so results can be interpreted fairly.

Data and conclusions

Organize results, look for patterns, and decide whether the evidence supports the hypothesis; unexpected results can lead to revision and repeated testing.

Try This in Class

Before selecting an answer at each science quiz orb, ask a pair to name the evidence or lab example that supports its choice. Finish by having students write one testable question and identify its independent variable.
