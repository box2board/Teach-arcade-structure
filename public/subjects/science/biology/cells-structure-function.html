Teaching cell structure and function

Use these points to frame instruction, discussion, or a quick debrief.

Cell theory

Living things are made of cells, cells are the basic unit of life, and new cells come from existing cells.

Cell boundaries

The cell membrane regulates what enters and leaves; plant cells also have a supportive cell wall.

Genetic control

The nucleus houses DNA in eukaryotic cells and directs many cell activities.

Energy and materials

Mitochondria release usable energy, chloroplasts capture light energy in plants, and cytoplasm supports cellular reactions.

Plant and animal cells

Both are eukaryotic and share many structures; chloroplasts, cell walls, and large central vacuoles distinguish typical plant cells.

Structure supports function

An organelle’s form, location, and internal features help it perform a specific job within the cell.
