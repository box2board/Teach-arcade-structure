[ela--writing-composition--writing-process-and-revision]
Prewriting|Exploring ideas and purpose before creating a first version.
Draft|Early version of a piece of writing.
Revision|Reworking ideas, organization, and meaning after a first version.
Editing|Correcting grammar, usage, and mechanics after larger changes.
Proofreading|Final check for remaining surface errors.
Audience|People whose needs shape a writer's choices.
Purpose|Reason for writing, such as to explain or persuade.
Organization|Arrangement of ideas and sections in a piece.
Feedback|Specific response identifying strengths or concerns in a draft.
Clarity|Ease with which a reader can understand the writing.
Word Choice|Selection of precise vocabulary for the intended meaning.
Introduction|Opening section establishing context and a controlling idea.
Conclusion|Closing section connecting back to the established point and its implications.
Claim|Position a writer may narrow after finding stronger evidence.
Reasoning|Explanation connecting supporting information to the central position.
Peer Review|Response from another student about a draft's effectiveness.
Redundancy|Unnecessary repetition of the same point.
Flow|Smooth movement of ideas and sentences through a passage.
Scope|Breadth of a claim that revision may narrow.
Tone|Writer's attitude that should fit the intended readers and purpose.
