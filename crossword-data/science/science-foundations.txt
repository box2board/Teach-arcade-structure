[scientific-method]
Observation|Information gathered directly with senses or scientific instruments.
Question|Focused inquiry that can guide a testable investigation.
Hypothesis|Testable proposed explanation or prediction.
Independent Variable|Factor deliberately changed by the investigator.
Dependent Variable|Outcome measured in response to a change.
Control Group|Comparison group that does not receive the tested treatment.
Constant|Condition kept the same across comparison groups.
Trial|One repetition of an experimental procedure.
Data|Recorded observations and measurements from an investigation.
Quantitative|Describes data expressed as numbers or measurements.
Qualitative|Describes observations about qualities rather than numerical amounts.
Procedure|Ordered steps used to carry out an investigation.
Sample Size|Number of subjects or observations included in a study.
Replication|Repeating an investigation to check whether results are consistent.
Measurement Error|Difference between a measured value and the quantity’s true value.
Outlier|Result unusually far from the rest of a data set.
Correlation|Relationship in which variables change together; alone it does not prove cause.
Causation|Relationship in which a change in one factor produces a change in another.
Controlled Experiment|Test that changes one factor while holding other relevant conditions steady.
Reproducibility|Ability of other investigators to repeat a study using its reported methods.
